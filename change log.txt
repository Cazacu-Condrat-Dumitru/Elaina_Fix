Version 1.1 Update Log:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Removed until they have their own models:
   - Hoki (Broom-san) companion, her portraits/icons, and the Hoki config options.
   - Traveler's Warded Pack (elena_pack). Items stored in it are lost in old saves.

Crash fixes:
   - Flight ending on its own read a DST-only global (TileGroupManager) -> crash.
   - Frost Elixir while holding an umbrella/lantern (no weapon component) -> crash.
   - Mana Potion / Primordial Water eaten by pigs or birds (no sanity) -> crash.
   - Traveler's Potion called a Shipwrecked-only function in the base game.

Bug fixes:
   - Gale Repulsion, Frost Nova and flight item magnetism now work (they searched
     for DST-only entity tags and never found anything).
   - Gale and Frost Nova only affect hostile creatures, never neutral animals or
     followers, and never knock enemies into water, walls or out of rooms.
   - SW/Hamlet: Elaina dealt ~175% damage because the level bonus was added as
     a full multiplier.
   - Flight keeps world, sea and Hamlet interior-wall collision (no more flying
     off the map or out of a room); no flying indoors, on a boat or while riding.
   - Mana Barrier cooldown is saved; it no longer vanishes or resets on reload.
   - R / L / Z no longer trigger while typing in the console or in menus.
   - Starter items are no longer re-created on every load (duplication).
   - Killing monsters no longer spawns extra brooms.
   - Frost Elixir keeps (and restores) the weapon's own effect, e.g. Bat Bat.
   - All mod items float in Shipwrecked instead of sinking.
   - Replaced missing sound events that were logged as FMOD errors.
   - Potion recipes: "nounlock" is now set correctly on SW/Hamlet.
   - Elaina's speech is her own copy instead of overwriting Wendy's lines.

Balance:
   - New EXP curve (100 + 20 x level): level 10 in a few days, 50 is reachable.
   - +1 max stats per level (was +2), +0.5% damage per level (was +1%).
   - Removed the permanent tier light and full temperature immunity; level 50
     now gives a 60-insulation weather ward instead.
   - Magic Wand: 25 damage (as in the original), each hit costs 1 sanity.
   - Broom: 34 damage by default (it's for flying, not fighting).
   - Hat: dapper like a Top Hat, no lightning immunity; still 100% waterproof
     by default, as in the original (configurable).
   - Gale Repulsion costs 10 sanity. Mana Barrier: once per day (was 3 minutes).
   - Ancient Grimoire (original: +40 sanity, unlimited): now once per day.
   - New Witchcraft tab to re-craft the broom, hat, wand and grimoire.

Lore:
   - Elaina has her own lines (confident, a little vain, a pragmatic traveler).
   - Removed invented "Volume/Animate" form names from the level tiers.

Version 1.0 Update Log:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Complete English Localization:
   - Translated all Chinese and foreign comments across all Lua scripts to clean English.
   - Cleaned all non-ASCII characters to prevent rendering glitches with Don't Starve bitmap fonts.

2. UI & Configuration Improvements:
   - Streamlined option labels in modinfo.lua to prevent button text clipping in the Don't Starve configuration menu.

3. Lore-Accurate Genius Witch Progression:
   - Added science_bonus = 1 to reflect Elaina's prodigy witch background.
   - Unlocked innate crafting knowledge for lantern, compass, and sewing_kit.
   - Added 3-tier Light Novel progression for Elaina (Apprentice -> Wandering Sorceress with ambient light and speed -> Legendary Arch-Witch with celestial starlight aura and Mana Shield thermal protection).

4. Broom Flight System:
   - Added aerial flight when pressing 'R' with the broom equipped at Level 10+.
   - Grants +60% movement speed, water gliding, obstacle phasing, and sparkling particles.

5. Hoki (Broom-san) Companion System:
   - Implemented dual-form transformation between Broom weapon and Hoki companion via the Ancient Grimoire.
   - Added autonomous ranged magic combat AI with homing projectiles.
   - Implemented sweets and magic potion upgrade systems.
   - Added progressive power evolution across levels (Apprentice -> Arch-Witch -> Celestial Starlight).
   - Created full open-source GitHub documentation (README.md, CREDITS.md, .gitignore).

6. Traveler's Warded Pack (elena_pack):
   - Expanded container capacity to 14 slots (Krampus Sack size) with configurable settings (8, 10, or 14 slots).
   - Signature enchanted backpack with 75% defense absorption, food preservation (fridge effect), and weather insulation against freezing/overheating.
   - Craftable from the Survival tab with basic materials (cut grass, twigs, rope).

7. Gale Repulsion Defense:
   - Added emergency starlight wind burst (Hotkey: 'Z') that pushes back and staggers nearby aggressive enemies.

8. Mana Barrier:
   - Added passive emergency barrier that prevents one-shot lethal blows, restoring emergency health and activating an automatic Gale Repulsion on a 3-minute cooldown.

9. Flight Resource Magnetism:
   - Added automatic ground resource vacuuming while flying on the broom.

10. Frost Nova:
   - Enhanced Frost Elixir (potion_icepowder) to unleash an instant AOE freezing wave that extinguishes fires and freezes nearby monsters.
